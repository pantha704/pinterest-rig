## Board descriptions (paste-ready, keyword-rich, no hashtags)

### 1. Cozy Home Decor & Aesthetic Room Ideas
> Cozy home decor ideas for small rooms and rented flats: warm lighting, layered texture, renter-friendly
> storage and the quiet home decor aesthetic that makes a space feel finished. Aesthetic room ideas,
> living room and bedroom inspiration, and small changes you can make this weekend — no drilling, no
> damage deposit drama.

*Why:* the top monetizable cluster in the harvest (prints, peel-and-stick wallpaper, home brands) and the
most evergreen — `wallpaper` alone holds 53 US / 68 GB with a 0.47 seasonality score, and the long tail
(`home decor aesthetic` +76% YoY, `living room aesthetic` +74%) is where a new account can still rank.

### 2. Wallpaper & Accent Wall Inspiration
> Wallpaper ideas for warm, layered rooms: heritage stripes, dark accent walls, peel-and-stick panels and
> autumn-toned patterns for hallways, bedrooms and reading nooks. Renter-friendly wallpaper and accent
> wall ideas that lift one wall without touching the other three.

*Why:* `wallpaper` is the biggest single head term in the decor harvest and the sub-niche with the widest
long tail (`wallpaper ideas` 79 GB, `wallpaper dark` 100 GB, `wallpaper autumn` 91 GB). Kept separate from
board 1 because wallpaper intent is visual and specific — a pin either shows a wall treatment or it does not.

### 3. Desk Setup & Study Aesthetic
> Desk setup ideas for small rooms and shared flats: cozy desk setups, warm lighting, screen risers,
> cable-tidy fixes and study aesthetic inspiration that makes working at home feel calmer. WFH desk setup
> ideas, small-space desk organisation and study-era corners that do not need a spare room.

*Why:* the clearest product-shaped cluster for a design-led account — `office desk setup` 98 US, `desk
setup ideas` 96 GB / 95 US (+76%), `study era` 94 US (+88%). Desk hardware (lamps, risers, keyboards) is
the affiliate angle once links start in batch 002+.

### 4. Planner, Journal & Study Printables
> Weekly planner templates, journal prompts and study printables you can print at home: undated weekly
> pages, habit trackers, journal page ideas and a planner system that survives past week three. Digital
> planner and printable planner inspiration for students, WFH weeks and anyone who abandons their planner
> by February.

*Why:* the digital-product heartland of the harvest: `weekly planner template` 86 GB (+43), `digital
planner` 70 US (+42), `planner inspiration` (+35%), `mini art journal` 74 US (+59%). Printables are the
first thing this account can monetise without inventory.

### 5. Cozy Autumn Home & Fall Decor
> Fall home decor ideas for cozy autumn evenings: warm bulbs, wool throws, dried stems, candlelight and
> simple autumn decor ideas for the living room, bedroom and entryway. Autumn home inspiration for small
> spaces, plus renter-friendly fall decorating that packs away again in December.

*Why:* live seasonality right now — `fall decor ideas for the home` 84 US, `fall home decor` 83 US, and
`fall home decor 2026` is a breakout at +79% year on year. Autumn terms carry the harvest's highest
seasonality scores (`autumn` 0.97, `fall wallpaper` 0.99), so this board earns traffic from September
through November.

### 6. Christmas Home Decor & Festive Ideas
> Christmas home decor ideas for a cozy, understated December: warm white lights, minimal Christmas tree
> styling, mantel greenery, festive tables and quiet Christmas wall art. Christmas decorating ideas for
> small homes and renters, plus gift-guide inspiration for the people who are hardest to buy for.

*Why planted in September:* every Christmas term sits near its annual low now and peaks at 100 in
Nov–Dec (`christmas home decor ideas`, `christmas table decor`, `cozy christmas living room` all peak
100; `christmas nails` is already 29 US / 41 GB with 0.99 seasonality). Pinterest surfaces seasonal
content 30–45 days ahead, so this board needs pins *before* the spike. This is the Q4 money board —
`christmas present ideas` 57 US (+50) is already climbing.

### 7. Printable Wall Art & Coloring Pages *(extra #1 — clearly supported)*
> Printable wall art and coloring pages for cozy homes: gallery-wall prints in warm neutral tones,
> autumn and Christmas printables, colouring pages for kids and detailed ones for grown-ups. Free

### 8. Gift Ideas & Small Joys *(extra #2 — clearly supported)*

## Deliberately not mapped (yet)

The harvest's *biggest* raw clusters are off-niche for an account whose only content engine is original
designs: **nails** (23 of the top 50 terms — `fall nails` 100 US), **fashion/outfits** (`autumn outfits`
78 GB) and **beauty/hair** (`hairstyles` 59 US). They need original photography and carry the lowest AOV
(`fall nails`) or the most saturated repin field (`outfit ideas`). Revisit fashion only if the account
adds a photo pipeline; nails are a traffic-only play worth a board once the design boards are indexed.
**Meal prep & budget food** (`meal prep snacks` 100 GB, `budget meals` 65 US) is a strong second-niche
candidate — it pairs with shopping-list printables rather than home decor, so it belongs to a separate
board map, not this one.

## How this board map is meant to be used

- **Batch-001 pins:** 20 pins, spread 3-3-3-3-3-2-2-1 across boards 1-8, **no links** (warm-up batch:
  build topical authority and on-platform saves first — Pinterest reads link-free saves as expertise,
  not advertising).
- **Cadence:** the rig's posting envelope is 1-2 pins/day ramping to 2-5/day, hours apart. Batch-001 is
  about a week of material at 3/day, which suits a fresh account better than a burst.
- **Ordering:** post boards 1-4 (evergreen) first for indexing, weaving in 5 (autumn) now and 6
  (Christmas) from early October so the pins are aged when the seasonal spike lands.
- **Spelling:** copy uses US spelling (`cozy`, `coloring`) because the US view is the bigger volume in
  every decor/planner cluster; UK readers accept it and GB-only terms are still targeted in titles.
- **Brand line on pins:** "Amber Hollow" + `@panther704` (set once via `BRAND` / `HANDLE` in
  `content/batch-001/tools/build_batch.py`; change there and re-run to re-render everything).
- **Board hygiene:** keep the eight boards tight; Pinterest rewards a board whose pins are all
  recognisably about the same thing. If a new sub-niche appears (e.g. small-space kitchens), give it its
  own board in batch 003 rather than diluting board 1.
