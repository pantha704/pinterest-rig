AFFILIATE PROGRAMS FOR A KOLKATA-BASED PINTEREST CREATOR — EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (2026). Full detail with inline official citations: /home/ubuntu/pinterest-rig/research/affiliate-programs.md
1. Pinterest's 2026 rules bind every choice: affiliate links are allowed in organic pins (not paid ads), must go to a product detail page, need clear #affiliate/#ad disclosure, no unsupported link shorteners, used in moderation — and product tagging is NOT available to India-based accounts.
2. Join in this order on signup day: EarnKaro first (live in minutes, no documents, Rs.10 payout floor), then Rakuten Advertising (open network that does not screen at signup, a Pinterest-named official partner, and the only global network paying INR direct deposit to an Indian bank by IFSC).
3. Then vCommission (native INR by NEFT/RTGS, Rs.1,000 floor, 2-4 day screening, PAN + invoice required) and Admitad (India entity, 809 advertisers incl. Myntra/AJIO/Tata CLiQ, INR 1,000 wallet minimum by bank transfer) — both reach Indian retail brands with no website needed.
4. Amazon Associates India pays native INR (Rs.1,000 NEFT, 24-hour session cookie) but its accepted-traffic list EXCLUDES Pinterest and it demands 3 qualifying sales within 180 days BEFORE approval — apply only once you have a >=10-post blog or a 500+ follower Instagram/YouTube profile.
5. Amazon Associates US is not an INR route: payout is a USD Amazon.com gift card (min $10) or a USD cheque (min $100), since direct deposit covers only 52 countries and India is not one; tax path is W-8BEN via the in-account Tax Interview, 30% withholding applies without a TIN/treaty claim, and PAN goes in the foreign-TIN field (India treaty rate 15%, but the characterisation of affiliate income is UNVERIFIED).
6. Never pin Amazon product images — Amazon's content licence is "solely on your Site" and forbids platforms that require sublicensing — whereas CJ Affiliate's official guide actually tells you to download product images and use them in pins, though CJ requires an active website, so gate it behind your blog.
7. Do not waste signup time on closed programs: ShareASale closed 6 Oct 2025 (now Awin) and ShopStyle Collective closed 31 Mar 2026 with sign-ups disabled — even though Pinterest still names ShopStyle as a partner.
8. LTK is the highest-value but hardest route: 5,000+ engaged followers on a public profile, 4+ months of shoppable content, Instagram authentication via a Facebook Business Page, brand commissions typically 10-25%, cookie 7-30 days — and its payout method/minimum for India is UNVERIFIED (dashboard-only, weekly payouts).
9. Awin has the best Pinterest documentation and product feeds but India eligibility is UNVERIFIED (tax-residency dropdown gate) and payment depends on Payoneer created inside your Awin account; impact.com is joinable now with INR by wire and a $10 floor but charges $10/month if it cannot pay you for 6 months.
10. The decisive cut is USD-only versus INR: EarnKaro, vCommission, Admitad, Amazon India and Rakuten pay INR natively, while Amazon US, Awin, CJ and LTK do not. Expect PAN everywhere and TDS (vCommission 1% individual / 2% company; Flipkart 5% under s.194H).
