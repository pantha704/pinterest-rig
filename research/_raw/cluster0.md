# India-Domestic Affiliate Programs — Official-Source Fact Sheet (compiled from live pages, current 2026)

Method note: every fact below is taken from the program's own site, help centre or terms page; the exact URL is given in parentheses. Nothing is sourced from third-party blogs. Facts I could not confirm officially are marked UNVERIFIED. Pre-2025-revision official documents are flagged where they are still the live official text.

Cross-cutting (Pinterest side, for pin monetisation): Pinterest's own Commercial and Branded Content Guidelines (Affiliate Guidelines section) require that you always follow the Paid Partnership Guidelines, are transparent about the commercial nature of your content and links, operate only one authentic Pinterest account, post original content, use affiliate links in moderation (not repetitively/at volume), and note that some link-shortener services are not currently supported (links may be blocked and must then be edited). No incentivised saving/exchange schemes. (https://policy.pinterest.com/en/commercial-and-branded-content-guidelines)

## 1. Amazon Associates India (Amazon.in Associates Central)

- Legal/network owner: Amazon Seller Services Private Ltd — the Operating Agreement states that 'We', 'us' or 'our' means Amazon Seller Services Private Ltd. (https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/operating/agreement)
- Program entry page: https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/ — tagline 'Earn up to 10% in affiliate fees from qualifying purchases and programs'. (https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/)
- Product categories available: all Amazon.in categories under the published Advertising Fee Schedule, except excluded products. Table 1 'Advertising Rates (Aug 2026)' lists: Apparel & Accessories / Shoes, Luggage & Bags, Watches / Beauty 10%; Books / Toys / Personal Care Appliances / Baby / Automotive / Sports 5.9%; Kitchen / Furniture / Home 5%; Echo & Alexa / Fire TV Devices 5%; Grocery / Amazon Fresh / Health & Personal Care 4.7%; Pet Products 4.7%; Mobile Accessories 4%; BISS / Lawn & Garden / Video Games 3.5%; Large Appliances / Televisions 3.5%; Personal Computers / Smart Watches / Electronics 3.5%; Bicycles & Heavy Gym Equipment / Tyres & Rims 2.5%; Data Storage Devices 2%; Mobile Phones 1%; Bill Payment & Recharges 30% capped at INR 3; All Other Categories 5%. (https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/operating/advertisingfees/)
- Commission range: effectively 0%-10% by category (many flagship phone models and Apple products are 0%; several phone models 0.5%). Bounties (flat INR, Table 2): Prime membership INR 100; Prime membership via PrimeVideo INR 100; Amazon Business INR 200; Audible Free Trial INR 150; Audible paid plan INR 150; Sell On Amazon completed registration INR 10; Sell On Amazon GST-validated INR 400; Prime Music first stream INR 100; Prime Music paid membership INR 100. Excluded products (no fee): Flight Bookings and Gift Cards, Video Gaming Consoles & Hardware, Prime Membership, Kindle eBooks, Gold & Silver Coins. (https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/operating/advertisingfees/)
- Cookie window / attribution: Session = 24 hours from click-through; the session ends on the first of (x) 24 hours elapsing, (y) the customer placing an order, or (z) the customer following a non-your Special Link. A Qualifying Purchase requires the item to be added to cart during that single session and the order placed no later than 89 days following the initial click-through. Help article: fees are earned on items placed in cart within 24 hours of arrival via your link; the fee still applies if the order is placed before the cart expires (usually after 90 days); the 24-hour window closes on order submission or when the customer re-enters Amazon via another associate's link. (https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/operating/agreement ; https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/node/topic/G9SMD8TQHFJ7728F)
- Payout methods (India publisher): NEFT direct deposit to an Indian bank account, or cheque; paid monthly, approximately 60 days after the end of each calendar month. (https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/node/topic/GKDG94FQSRXSJCGK)
- Minimum payout threshold: INR 1,000 for NEFT; INR 2,500 for cheque. Associates can set their own higher cheque minimum (e.g. INR 5,000), effective if submitted by the first day of the payment month. Currency: INR. (https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/node/topic/GKDG94FQSRXSJCGK ; https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/node/topic/GP8Z3AZ27ZFHTEUL)
- No invoice required: Amazon states it does not expect Associates to provide an invoice to receive payment; payment is automatic once the minimum threshold is reached — but check whether local law requires you to invoice anyway. (https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/node/topic/GLT6NH3K387FSQLD)
- Signup requirements — is a website/blog/social channel required: yes. Homepage FAQ: 'Bloggers, publishers and content creators with a qualifying website or mobile app can participate in this program.' (https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/)
- Signup requirements — traffic/approval mechanics: after signup you must drive at least three qualified sales within the first 180 days, or the application is not approved; personal orders do not qualify. All declared Sites (websites, mobile apps, social pages) are reviewed: websites must have robust original content (rule of thumb: at least 10 posts), be owned by you, and be recent (generally within the last 60 days); social pages must be established with a substantive number of organic followers/likes (in most cases at least 500) and be publicly available (no closed/hidden groups, no hidden follower counts). Accepted social networks are only: Facebook (open group/fan pages, not personal pages), Instagram, Twitter, YouTube, TikTok and Twitch.tv — Pinterest is NOT on this list. Applications cannot be reassessed once rejected; you must submit the exact social page URL (not just the platform domain). (https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/node/topic/G8TW5AE9XL2VX9VM/)
- Referral/invite needed: no — open application, reviewed on the 3-sales-in-180-days trigger. (https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/node/topic/G8TW5AE9XL2VX9VM/)
- Approval difficulty: hard for a no-site, Pinterest-only publisher — the review page lists only the six social networks above, so a Pinterest-only applicant has no accepted traffic source to declare, and the 3-qualifying-sales requirement must be met anyway. (https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/node/topic/G8TW5AE9XL2VX9VM/)
- Official marketing assets / product images / branded creative: the license to copy and display Content (data, images, text, links, widgets) is granted 'solely on your site', and Amazon Marks may be used only as part of the Content and only per the Trademark Guidelines. You may not add to, delete from or alter Content (resizing a graphic is allowed only if original proportions are maintained). You may not sell, resell, redistribute, sublicense or transfer Content, and you may not use Content on any application, platform, site or service (including social networking sites) that requires you to sublicense rights in the Content. Content may be used solely to send users to Amazon. (https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/operating/agreement ; https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/operating/participation/)
- Attribution disclosure required: you must clearly and prominently state 'As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.' (https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/operating/agreement)
- India-specific eligibility caveats: advertising fee is inclusive of all taxes; you must raise valid GST-compliant invoices and report them so Amazon can take input tax credit; if you are an Indian resident, fees are subject to income-tax withholding at the rate stipulated under applicable law (TDS); you must provide PAN and tax information or Amazon may hold fees in abeyance; nil/reduced withholding certificate can be applied; non-residents must provide additional documentation; governing law is India with exclusive jurisdiction of courts at Bangalore; fees may be withheld until the NEFT minimum of INR 1,000 is met. (https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/operating/agreement)
- Note on document vintage: the Operating Agreement page carries the stamp 'Updated: November, 2021' but is the live official agreement; the Advertising Fee Schedule table is headed 'Aug 2026' and the help pages carry 2025-2026 footers. (https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/operating/agreement ; https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/operating/advertisingfees/)

## 5. Are Flipkart Affiliate, Myntra, AJIO, Tata CLiQ and similar Indian retail affiliate programs currently open to new Indian publishers?

### Flipkart Affiliate Program (direct portal affiliate.flipkart.com)
- Legal owner: Flipkart Internet Private Limited (the Operating Agreement on the affiliate site states 'we', 'us' or 'our' means Flipkart Internet Private Limited or any of our affiliates). (https://affiliate.flipkart.com/terms-and-conditions)
- Openness (checked on the live portal): the homepage exposes only Login / forgotten-password; there is no Sign-Up or Register link in the page navigation, and the direct registration paths return HTTP 404 — https://affiliate.flipkart.com/register and /getting-started both returned 404 at the time of checking; the API documentation still references a registration URL (https://affiliate.flipkart.com/registerme) but that page renders empty. There is no official notice on the portal stating that registrations are paused/closed, so the current official position is best described as: no public sign-up route is exposed on the official portal — the widely repeated claim that signups were 'paused w.e.f. 5 May 2018' is UNVERIFIED (secondary sources only). (https://affiliate.flipkart.com/ ; https://affiliate.flipkart.com/register ; https://affiliate.flipkart.com/api-docs/af_register.html)
- Product categories and commission: official commissions page headed 'REFERRAL PAYOUTS FOR ORDERS IN SEPTEMBER 2026' — Fashion & Lifestyle 8%; Books & General Merchandise 5%; Home 3%; Furniture 3%; Core Electronics 3%; Emerging Electronics 3%; Large Appliances 3%; Small Home Appliances 2%; Grocery (Flipkart Supermart, live in listed cities including Kolkata) 2%; Mobile Phones Tier A 1%, Tier B 0.5%, Tier C 0%, any other mobile phones 0%; Flipkart Gift Card 0%; Gemstones, Gold & Silver Coins 0.1%. Excluded from payouts: Flipkart First subscription, Value Added Services, iOS & Windows App Installs and other notified products. The FAQ advertises 'earn up to 12%', but the September 2026 published table tops out at 8% for fashion. (https://affiliate.flipkart.com/commissions ; https://affiliate.flipkart.com/faqs ; https://affiliate.flipkart.com/)
- Cookie window: 30 minutes — the FAQ explains that for a sale to earn a referral fee the customer must click through and within a single session add the product to cart; the session ends on the first of: 30 minutes elapsing from the click, the customer placing an order, or the customer following a third-party link tagged for Flipkart. Fees are paid only after order, payment and shipping. (https://affiliate.flipkart.com/faqs)
- Payout methods (India publisher) and minimum threshold: two modes — Gift Voucher (EGV) or Electronic Fund Transfer (EFT). 'If affiliates select payment via gift voucher, Flipkart will accrue and withhold referral fees until the total amount due is at least Rs 25,000.00. If affiliates select payment via Electronic Fund Transfer (EFT), Flipkart will accrue and withhold referral fees until the total amount due is at least Rs 25,000.00.' Currency: INR. (https://affiliate.flipkart.com/payment-information ; the Operating Agreement uses the same INR 25,000 thresholds for gift card and cheque/EFT.) (https://affiliate.flipkart.com/terms-and-conditions)
- Payment timing: monthly, approximately 60 days following the end of the calendar month; because of the 30-day cancellation/return policy, payments are made after 30-60 days from order date; earnings show after delivery. (https://affiliate.flipkart.com/payment-information ; https://affiliate.flipkart.com/terms-and-conditions)
- Signup/KYC requirements: PAN-based KYC is mandatory — docs required: Payee Name (same as PAN), PAN details, IFSC code, bank account number, mailing address, contact number; organisations additionally need incorporation certificate and GST certificate. Website-based ('Your site') participation is the model (drive traffic from your website/mobile site/mobile app); application is reviewed for suitability. Free to join ('absolutely no costs involved in joining'). (https://affiliate.flipkart.com/payment-information ; https://affiliate.flipkart.com/ ; https://affiliate.flipkart.com/faqs)
- Tax/TDS: 5% TDS on all payments via EFT and on Gift Vouchers if PAN is provided (referred to as commission under Section 194H of the Income Tax Act); if PAN is not provided, gift-voucher payments are subject to 20% TDS withholding. (https://affiliate.flipkart.com/payment-information ; https://affiliate.flipkart.com/terms-and-conditions)
- Asset/social restrictions relevant to pins: the Operating Agreement prohibits promoting affiliate links (directly or indirectly) via paid ads on search engine result pages and social media websites, and lists other prohibited uses (misleading offers, links on sites offering referral programs/MLM, illegal download sites); the agreement also allows Flipkart to set a Monthly Limit on referral fees.
