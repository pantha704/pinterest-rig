## PART A — Amazon Associates US (affiliate-program.amazon.com)

### A(i) Can an India-based person join the US programme?

- **No territorial bar for a non-US applicant is published; the US Operating Agreement restricts only sanctioned persons/export rules.** Warranties clause, verbatim: “you will not participate in the Associates Program or use any other Service Offering if you are the subject of U.S. sanctions or of sanctions consistent with U.S. law imposed by the governments of the country where you are using any Service Offering” and “you will comply with all U.S. export and re-export restrictions, and applicable non-US export and re-export restrictions consistent with U.S. law”. Source: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/agreement
- **Non-US persons are expressly contemplated as Associates of the US programme.** Associates Program Participation Requirements, verbatim: “If you are a non-US person participating in the Associates Program, you agree that unless you have otherwise notified us in your tax information you will perform all services under the Agreement outside the United States.” Source: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies
- **Amazon’s own help text assumes non-US associates of the US programme exist.** Verbatim: “The US Internal Revenue Service requires Amazon to withhold up to 30% from non-US associates earning referral commissions in the US unless the associate can provide a completed W-8BEN with a valid claim of treaty benefits.” Same page, verbatim: “Being a member of the Amazon Associates US program does not mean that you supply your services both inside and outside the United States. (i.e.) if you are an Associate in the UK, posting links to a US based site, does not constitute US sourced income.” Source: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GYJB2LE2AB473W2L
- **Corroborating official artefact: the US programme pays associates whose bank accounts sit in 52 non-US countries** (see A(iii)) — i.e. US-programme membership is not limited to US residents. Source: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/G8VUMS6GTBCR9RGV
- **India-specific “yes” statement: UNVERIFIED.** Amazon publishes no country-eligibility list for the US programme (no official page found stating that India residents may or may not join affiliate-program.amazon.com), and India is absent from the 52-country international bank-transfer list. The only defensible reading of the official documents is: nothing prohibits it, the agreement contemplates non-US persons, but Amazon has not published an India-specific confirmation.
- **Hard practical gate for a Pinterest-only applicant: Pinterest is NOT on Amazon’s accepted social-network list.** Verbatim: “We currently only accept the following social networks: Facebook (including open group pages and fan pages, but excluding personal pages), Instagram, Twitter, YouTube, Tik Tok and Twitch.tv. Your application must clearly list your social media page’s exact URL … Your social network page or group must be established, with a substantive number of organic followers/likes (in most cases, at least 500).” Source: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/G8TW5AE9XL2VX9VM
- **Content bar for the declared Site:** “All Sites must have robust original content (even when advertising is removed) – a good rule of thumb is at least 10 posts. They must be publicly available (for example, they should not be a closed group, or behind a paywall).” Source: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/G8TW5AE9XL2VX9VM
- **A social-media account CAN be a “Site” in the agreement’s own words:** “The Associates Program permits you to monetize your website, social media user-generated content, online software application, or Alexa skill (referred to here as your “Site”)”. Source: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/agreement
- **A separate India programme exists with INR payouts and its own agreement** (useful because the US-programme path cannot pay INR): https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/operating/agreement and https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/node/topic/GGD9H76RMDDNEWAE

### A(ii) Tax form: W-8BEN vs W-9, where submitted, India treaty, TIN/PAN

- **The form is chosen by the online Tax Interview, not by you.** Verbatim: “The Tax Interview is designed to obtain information required to complete an IRS W-9, W-8 or 8233 form to determine if your payments are subject to IRS Form 1099-MISC or 1042-S reporting.” Source: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com.au/help/node/topic/GYJB2LE2AB473W2L (identical guide is served from the US Associates help; see also Amazon’s Tax Information Interview Guide: https://developer.amazon.com/tax-interview/help?locale=en_US&nodeId=201588330)
- **W-9 = US persons only.** Amazon’s own form-applicability table: “W-8BEN — Non-U.S. individuals claiming foreign status and/or treaty benefits”; “W-9 — U.S. persons, including US citizens, US resident individuals, and US entities.” And in the interview text: “This form should be completed by U.S. persons only.” Sources: https://developer.amazon.com/tax-interview/help?locale=en_US&nodeId=201588330 and https://affiliate-program.amazon.com.au/help/node/topic/GYJB2LE2AB473W2L
- **⇒ For a Kolkata-resident individual the correct form is W-8BEN** (non-US individual, beneficial owner). Supports: the Amazon US help sentence naming “a completed W-8BEN with a valid claim of treaty benefits”: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GYJB2LE2AB473W2L
- **Where it is submitted (official click-path):** “1. Log in to Amazon Associates https://affiliate-program.amazon.com 2. Hover over your email address at the top of the page 3. Click on Account Settings 4. Click on View/Provide Tax Information to review or update specific tax information 5. Click “Take Interview””; “Once the tax interview is complete, you can follow the status of your submission on your account page under “Payment Information.”” Source: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GYJB2LE2AB473W2L
- **Default withholding is 30% and the treaty claim is what reduces it.** Verbatim: “If you are a resident of a country that has an active Double Taxation treaty with the United States, you may be eligible to receive a reduction in the statutory U.S. withholding tax rate of 30% if you qualify as a resident under Article 4 of the treaty between your country of residence and the United States.” Source: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com.au/help/node/topic/GYJB2LE2AB473W2L
- **TIN requirement, verbatim:** “It is not necessary to have a TIN in order to determine if you are a U.S. or non-U.S. person for U.S. income tax purposes. However, any reduction of the statutory withholding tax rate (30%) applicable to your U.S. source payments will not apply until you can provide a TIN.” If income is effectively connected (US permanent establishment) you “must provide a U.S. tax identification number (TIN)”. Source: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com.au/help/node/topic/GYJB2LE2AB473W2L
- **What counts as a TIN for a non-US person per Amazon:** “For non-U.S. persons, this may be an ITIN (generally for individuals) or EIN (generally for companies).” Source: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GYJB2LE2AB473W2L
- **India treaty mechanics.** The US–India Convention Article 12 caps tax on royalties and fees for included services at 15% of the gross amount (10% for the equipment-rental sub-case). Source: https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-trty/india.pdf . IRS Table 1 (Rev. May 2023) lists India royalties at 15. Source: https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-lbi/tax-treaty-table-1.pdf . **Whether affiliate referral commissions are characterized as royalties/FIS (15%), business profits (Article 7 — potentially no US withholding absent a US permanent establishment), or something else is a characterization judgement; no Amazon document states an India rate. Treat the exact India result as UNVERIFIED — confirm with a tax adviser before relying on any single rate.**
- **PAN guidance:** India’s official tax identification number is the PAN — “TIN in India is officially called Permanent Account Number (PAN)”, OECD TIN portal: https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/topics/policy-issue-focus/aeoi/india-tin.pdf ; Indian government PAN page: https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/pan . IRS Form W-8BEN instructions require, for treaty claims, either an SSN/ITIN on line 5 or a foreign TIN (FTIN) on line 6a; line 6b exists for persons not legally required to obtain an FTIN — for an Indian resident the PAN is the FTIN to enter. Source: https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iw8ben . **Amazon’s help never names “PAN” and no official Amazon page confirms the interview’s India field mapping — UNVERIFIED at the Amazon layer; the foreign-TIN field is the intended place.**
- **Reporting you receive as a non-US payee:** “The IRS requires Amazon, to issue an information statement called the 1042-S form to Non US payees. … The Form 1042-S will be issued by March 15th of the year following payment.” (US persons instead get 1099 at USD 600+.) Source: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GYJB2LE2AB473W2L
- **Fallback if a US TIN is ever needed:** ITIN via IRS Form W-7, “usually takes 4–6 weeks”. Source: https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iw8ben

### A(iii) Payment methods, minimum thresholds, and whether INR bank deposit is genuinely supported

- **Three methods only: direct deposit / Amazon gift card / cheque.** Operating Agreement (Commission Income Statement §6) options, verbatim: “Option 1: Payment by Direct Deposit… If you select this option, we reserve the right to hold commission income until the total amount due to you reaches the minimum stated in the Payment Minimum Chart.” / “Option 2: Payment by Amazon Gift Card… We reserve the right to hold the portion of payments that exceeds the maximum stated in the Payment Chart until you provide an alternate payment method.” / “Option 3: Payment by Check… and to deduct a processing fee as stated in the Payment Minimum Chart from each check we send to you.” Payments are made “approximately 60 days following the end of each calendar month”. Source: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies
- **US Payment Minimum Chart (official, same page):** Option 1 Deposit — minimum 10 USD; Option 2 Gift Card — minimum 10 USD, maximum 2000 USD; Option 3 Check — minimum 100 USD; Check Processing Fee — 15 USD. Source: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies
- **Payment FAQ, verbatim quote:** “We pay you commission income on a monthly basis. Approximately 60 days after the end of each month, we will either send you a cheque, send you an Amazon Gift Certificate or deposit your commissions directly into your bank account.” and “If you are being paid by Amazon Gift Certificate or bank transfer, we will not send payment until the total amount due is at least USD 10. If you are being paid by cheque, we will not send payment until the total amount due is at least USD 100.” Source: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GKDG94FQSRXSJCGK
- **Is bank deposit to an Indian bank account supported? No — not under the US programme’s international transfer scheme.** Verbatim: “Amazon Associates members participating in an Amazon Associate Program in the US, UK, DE, FR, IT, ES, or CA, with a bank account in either the US (USD), United Kingdom (GBP and EUR), Eurozone (EUR)– a total of 52 countries, can now receive their international incomes by bank transfer in their local currency to their bank account.” The published country table contains only the United States (USD), United Kingdom (GBP or EUR) and an explicit Eurozone/Aland list (EUR). **India is not in the list.** Source: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/G8VUMS6GTBCR9RGV
- **What a non-US address with no direct-deposit access actually gets (official):** “If your address is outside the United States, and direct deposit is, therefore, unavailable to you, we will waive the $15 fee. We will not send payment until the total amount due is at least $100.” (cheque option). Source: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GGD9H76RMDDNEWAE
- **Net answer for Kolkata + US programme:** gift card (min 10 USD, capped at 2000 USD per payment) or paper cheque (min 100 USD, 15 USD fee waived when direct deposit is unavailable). Payout currency is the Amazon Site’s default currency — USD for amazon.com: “We will pay Standard Commission Income and Special Commission Income in the default currency for an Amazon Site approximately 60 days following the end of each calendar month…” Source: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies . **⇒ No INR payout and no Indian-bank direct deposit via the US programme.** (Amazon’s separate India programme does pay INR: direct deposit minimum 1000 INR, cheque minimum 2500 INR — https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/node/topic/GGD9H76RMDDNEWAE ; payment for month M lands in late month M+2, e.g. “Payment for January is made in late March” — https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/node/topic/G63DR893K4DH55XZ)

### A(v) Are Amazon product images permitted in pins / social posts?

- **The licence is to display Program Content on YOUR Site — and “Site” expressly includes social-media UGC.** Operating Agreement §1 defines Site as “your website, social media user-generated content, online software application, or Alexa skill”. Source: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/agreement
- **IP License grant, verbatim:** “we hereby grant you a limited, revocable, non-transferable, non-sublicensable, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to: (a) copy and display Program Content solely on your Site”. Program Content includes the “data, images, text, link formats, widgets, links, marketing content, and other linking tools”. Sources: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies and https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/agreement
- **Images must come from Amazon’s own tools** (“You may obtain Product Advertising Content by making calls to Creators API or PA API”, or SiteStripe on Associates Central). Source: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies
- **No alteration:** “You will not add to, delete from, or otherwise alter any Program Content in any way, including by adding additional information, except that you may resize Program Content consisting of a graphic image in a manner that maintains the original proportions of the image”. Source: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies
- **No image caching / 24-hour rule:** “You will not store or cache Product Advertising Content consisting of an image, but you may store a link to Product Advertising Content consisting of an image for up to 24 hours.” Source: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies
- **Every image must carry a link to the relevant Amazon page:** “You will link each use of Product Advertising Content to, and only to, the relevant page of an Amazon Site (for example, the relevant Product detail page …)”. Source: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies
- **No transfer/sublicensing of Program Content to a platform that demands rights in it:** “you will not sell, resell, redistribute, sublicense, or transfer any Program Content … For example, you will not use, or enable, or facilitate the use of Program Content within advertising outside of your Site or on or within any application, platform, site, or service (including social networking sites) that requires you to sublicense or otherwise give any rights in or to any Program Content to any other person or entity, nor will you create links formatted with your Associates tag for, or display such links on, a site that is not your Site.” Source: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies
- **No implied endorsement:** “You will not use any Program Content, including any name or likeness embodied in that Program Content, in a manner that implies a person’s or company’s endorsement or sponsorship of… any product, service, party, or cause (including by placing unrelated third party materials in close proximity to Program Content).” Source: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies
- **Trademark guidelines ban offline use and constrain context:** marks may be used “only by display on your Site with the purpose of advertising availability of products on an Amazon site, with a corresponding Special Link to that site”, and “You may not use or display the Marks … in offline material or email”. Source: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies
- **Disclosure is mandatory on the social account itself:** “For social media user-generated content, this statement must be associated with your account.” Link-level disclosure must be “1. Clear. A clear disclosure could be as simple as “(paid link)”, “#ad”, or “#CommissionsEarned”. 2. Conspicuous. It should be placed near any affiliate link or product review in a location that customers will notice easily.” Source: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GPXFHVYZMTGPUMPE
- **A dedicated permission to place Amazon product images in PINS: UNVERIFIED.** No Amazon page names Pinterest in either direction (neither expressly permitting nor forbidding pinning Program Content). The permissions above are Site-scoped and tool-scoped, so the defensible position is: images obtained from Amazon’s approved tools may be displayed on a declared Site, and Pinterest image use is only clean if the Pinterest profile is a Site Amazon has accepted — which the accepted-social-network list (Facebook/Instagram/Twitter/YouTube/TikTok/Twitch) currently does not include. Sources: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/G8TW5AE9XL2VX9VM and https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies
- **Pinterest’s own Amazon integration is a different route and needs the Amazon Influencer Program:** it requires “a Pinterest business account”, being “a participant in the Amazon Influencer Program (AIP)” and having “an Amazon Storefront”; links then get affiliate tags applied automatically with an automatic affiliate disclosure. Source: https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/connect-amazon-and-pinterest . Note AIP itself is “a country specific program that is available in select countries” — http://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies . Whether India is an AIP country: UNVERIFIED.

### A(vi) The 180-day rules (and what the “3 sales / 180 days” actually is)

- **First 180 days = audition window, verbatim:** “Once you’ve applied, you have 180 days to refer a sale through one of your Associates links.” and “Once you have referred three qualifying sales, we’ll evaluate your application within a day or two and reply to you with our decision.” and “Your application can be withdrawn if there were not enough qualifying sales for 180 days after your application was submitted. We’re unable to reinstate your account or Associates ID after it’s been rejected.” Source: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/G7MJTPEP9NC3YKMG
- **Same rule stated in the review-process page, verbatim:** “After you sign up, our Associates team will check your application once you’ve driven qualified sales (we require at least three within the first 180 days). Please note that personal orders do not qualify.” Source: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/G8TW5AE9XL2VX9VM
- **⇒ The correct reading of the “3 sales per 180 days” rule: it is a 3-qualifying-sales-in-the-first-180-days review gate (pre-approval), not a rolling maintenance requirement.** After acceptance the only continuing inactivity rule published is dormancy: “If you have not earned any commission income in the 3 years prior to any given calendar month, then on the first day of that calendar month we may charge you an account maintenance fee that will be deducted from your unpaid accrued commission income. That account maintenance fee will be the lesser of $10 or the amount of unpaid accrued commission income in your account. … Your account will be closed for dormancy when we take this action.” Source: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/G7MJTPEP9NC3YKMG (mirrored in the Commission Income Statement §6: “If at any time there has been no substantial activity on your account for at least 3 years, then we will have the right, with 7 days’ written notice to withhold the accrued commission income for your inactive account…”) — https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies
- **Any claim that account closure is triggered by failing to keep making 3 sales every 180 days post-acceptance: UNVERIFIED** — that language does not appear in the October 2025 Operating Agreement or the April 2026 Program Policies.
- **Termination is otherwise at-will with 7 days’ notice, and Amazon can terminate immediately for the listed causes.** Operating Agreement §6. Source: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/agreement
- **Re-application note:** rejected-for-unsuitable-content may reapply after fixing content; rejected for any other reason or terminated for violation requires Amazon’s advance authorisation. Source: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies

## Bottom line for the Kolkata / Pinterest use case

1. **Amazon Associates US is the only one of the three that is genuinely open today**, but with two hard caveats for this person: the accepted-social-network list does not include Pinterest, so a declared blog/website (or an accepted network) is effectively required; and **INR payout via the US programme is not supported** — the payout options are USD gift card (≥ $10) or USD cheque (≥ $100, $15 fee waived when direct deposit is unavailable). Direct deposit requires a bank account in the US/UK/Eurozone (52-country list, India excluded).
2. **Tax path is W-8BEN via the in-account Tax Interview** (Account Settings → View/Provide Tax Information → Take Interview), 30% default withholding absent a valid treaty claim, 1042-S issued for non-US payees. PAN is India’s TIN and is the foreign-TIN field input; characterization of affiliate income under the US–India treaty (15% royalties vs business profits) is not stated by Amazon and must be confirmed with an adviser.
3. **LTK is worth an application** (application-based, 5,000+ followers on one public profile, daily/twice-weekly shoppable content, 4+ months of content, Facebook Business Page needed for Instagram authentication; 7–30 day typical cookie; 7–11%+ net commission bands; official 10–25% headline range) — but **payout method/threshold for India is not published publicly** and must be confirmed inside the creator dashboard.
4. **ShopStyle Collective is dead** — ShopStyle Inc. dba Collective Voice shut the creator network on March 31, 2026; sign-ups disabled; final payment July 19, 2026; accounts closed July 31, 2026. Do not shortlist it.
