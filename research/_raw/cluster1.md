# Affiliate network fact sheet — India (Kolkata) eligibility + Pinterest relevance
Scope: 2025–2026 only. Every fact below is followed by the exact official source URL in parentheses. Anything I could not confirm from an official (network / help-centre / Pinterest) page is marked **UNVERIFIED**.

## Cross-cutting context (Pinterest platform rules)
- Pinterest's official affiliate partners are **Rakuten, LTK, ShopStyle and Amazon Associates US** — “Pinterest isn't part of the affiliate agreements, so you'll need to choose an affiliate network” (https://create.pinterest.com/blog/affiliate-marketing-link-basics/).
- Pinterest requires the pin's destination affiliate link to “go directly to a shop's product detail page”; some link shorteners are not supported; commercial content must be disclosed (https://create.pinterest.com/blog/affiliate-marketing-link-basics/).
- Pinterest **product tagging** is available only in US, CA, UK, DE, FR, IT, ES, AT, CH, NL, AU, BR, MX, JP — **India is not in that list** (https://create.pinterest.com/blog/affiliate-marketing-link-basics/).
- Awin, CJ and Rakuten each publish Pinterest-specific guidance; impact.com documents Pinterest as a connectable promotional channel but publishes no Pinterest how-to article (**UNVERIFIED** that an impact.com Pinterest how-to exists).

## 1. Awin
- **Legal/network owner / contract entity:** “AWIN LTD Publisher Terms applies if: You have a non-EU tax residence (including UK) and you work on program(s) on the Awin network”; AWIN AG terms apply to EU tax residents; separate Awin Brazil and Awin Mexico terms exist — so a Kolkata-based publisher contracts with **AWIN LTD** (https://www.awin.com/us/publisher-terms).
- **Product categories:** publisher promotional types include “Content creators & influencers”, comparison engines, cashback, discount-code, editorial, media content etc.; publishers pick sectors/subsectors that advertisers filter on (examples given: Retail, Finance, Travel; subsectors Clothing, Health & Beauty) (https://success.awin.com/articles/en_US/Knowledge/What-is-promotional-type-and-what-should-I-select ; https://help.awin.com/docs/searching-for-partners-in-the-new-awin-platform).
- **Commission range:** Not published by Awin; rates are per advertiser / commission group and viewable in-platform via the commission timeline (https://success.awin.com/articles/en_US/Knowledge/How-can-i-view-advertiser-commission-rate). Awin's own FAQ: “Exactly how much money you make depends on several factors unique to you. There is no limit to what you can earn” (https://www.awin.com/us/getting-started-sas).
- **Cookie window / attribution:** “Most affiliate cookies are valid for 30 days”; Awin also runs first-party, server-to-server and cookieless tracking; soft-click cookies “usually expire at the end of the session”; third-party-cookie blocking can lose transactions (https://success.awin.com/s/article/Cookie-Tracking-Publisher-FAQs). No per-programme network-wide cookie figure is published (**UNVERIFIED** that every advertiser uses 30 days).
- **Payout methods (India):** Method is auto-determined by payout currency + bank location — SEPA (EUR to EU/UK), BACS (GBP to UK), ACH (USD to US), domestic rails for DK/SE/NO/PL/CH/BR/MX; **all cross-border payments are processed via Payoneer Global Bank Transfer**, “either in local currency or through international wire transfer using the SWIFT network”; you must register with Payoneer from inside your Awin account and “It is not possible to link an already existing Payoneer account to Awin” (https://success.awin.com/s/article/what-are-the-available-payment-methods ; https://success.awin.com/articles/en_US/Knowledge/Payments-through-Payoneer-FAQs).
- **Awin native payout currencies:** “Awin supports ten currencies: USD, EUR, GBP, AUD, CAD, SEK, DKK, NOK, CHF, and PLN. Through Payoneer, you can choose to receive payments in: Your local currency; USD, EUR, or GBP (in select regions)” — **INR is not a native Awin currency**; INR would only arise as a Payoneer local-currency payout and Awin does not name India in this article (**UNVERIFIED**) (https://success.awin.com/s/article/International-Payment-Method-FAQs).
- **Minimum payout threshold:** “£20 for GBP payments / €20 for Euro payments / $20 for USD payments / For other currencies, the threshold is equivalent to $20”; if unset, defaults apply: 50 GBP/EUR/USD/CHF/AUD/BRL, 100 CAD/PLN, 500 SEK/NOK, “or the equivalent for other currencies”. Balance must reach the threshold by the 15th or last day of the month (https://success.awin.com/s/article/What-are-the-payment-thresholds). Payments are issued on the 1st or 15th of each month (https://success.awin.com/s/article/Understanding-Transaction-Status-and-Payment-Process).
- **Payoneer fees/timing:** no monthly or annual fee; ~1.75% FX fee if conversion occurs; local-currency payout 1–3 business days vs SWIFT 3–5 business days, and banks/intermediaries may take as much as 6% on SWIFT (https://success.awin.com/s/article/Payoneer-for-Awin-Publishers-FAQs ; https://success.awin.com/articles/en_US/Knowledge/Receiving-payments-via-Payoneer-Local-currency-vs-wire-transfer).
- **Signup requirements:** must be 18+ and “Have a promotional space where you plan to share affiliate links. This can be a website, blog, social media channel, newsletter, or another approved platform.” **No minimum traffic or audience requirement is stated.** Tax info and bank details are needed later, not at signup (https://success.awin.com/s/article/What-are-the-requirements-for-joining-the-Awin-network). Social-only signup is supported via an Instagram-account influencer sign-up path (https://success.awin.com/articles/en_US/Knowledge/How-can-I-get-started-at-Awin-as-an-influencer).
- **Approval difficulty (new publisher):** applications “are generally reviewed automatically in near real-time”; if incomplete or more information is needed, “your application will be reviewed within 2 business days” (https://success.awin.com/s/article/What-are-the-requirements-for-joining-the-Awin-network). Rejection triggers: missing/inconsistent information, unverifiable name/address/website, promotional-space description lacking clarity, no proof of domain ownership, fake/impersonated/inappropriate accounts; after rejection “Your application data is stored securely for 60 days” and you can reapply with the same email, but “Previous rejections may impact future applications, including through fingerprinting or device/email history”; escalations go to approvals@awin.com (https://success.awin.com/s/article/Why-was-my-network-application-rejected).
- **Pinterest usage (officially documented):** “You can publish affiliate links on Pinterest in two ways: by creating new pins with your affiliate links as the URL, or by replacing existing pins' URLs with your links”, and links can be shortened with the MyAwin browser extension (https://success.awin.com/s/article/How-do-I-promote-affiliate-links-on-social-media-platform).
- **Product image feeds:** “Any Awin publisher can access product data feeds” (product names, descriptions, prices, images, deep links), aimed mainly at comparison sites, shopping portals, review sites and daily-deal platforms; download limits of 5 concurrent and 5 duplicate requests per hour; configured via Create-a-Feed / Enhanced Feeds (https://success.awin.com/articles/en_US/Knowledge/Product-Data-Feed-FAQ ; https://success.awin.com/articles/en_US/Knowledge/what-kinds-of-promotional-methods-are-available). Awin publishes **no statement authorising or prohibiting republishing feed images as Pinterest pins — UNVERIFIED**.
- **Disclosure obligation:** publishers must disclose affiliate relationships (#ad / #sponsored etc.) per FTC and EU rules; Awin offers the adMission disclosure tool (https://success.awin.com/articles/en_US/Knowledge/What-are-the-guidelines-for-ads-on-social-media).
- **India-specific eligibility:** **https://www.awin.com/in and https://www.awin.com/in/publishers return HTTP 404 — there is no Awin India site** (verified by direct HTTP request, Sept 2026). Awin's tax FAQ is the gate: if your country is not in the Tax Residency dropdown, “Awin's services aren't available there, and you won't be able to proceed” — the list itself is not published, so **India's presence in it is UNVERIFIED** (https://success.awin.com/s/article/Publisher-Tax-Details-FAQs).
- **Tax forms for India:** tax residency + business type (Registered Company / Sole Trader / Micro Entrepreneur-Brazil) + company name + VAT ID or, under “Other Countries”, “Provide your local tax number”; a Tax Verification Document (e.g. a certificate issued by your tax authority) may be requested; “If you don't have a tax number assigned, use another ID”. W-9 is mandatory only for US tax residency (https://success.awin.com/s/article/Publisher-Tax-Details-FAQs). **PAN, GST and Indian TDS are not mentioned anywhere in Awin's official documentation (UNVERIFIED).**

## 2. Impact.com
- **Legal/network owner:** two-layer contract — the Partner Program Agreement accepted at signup, plus a per-brand contract: “Your contract with a brand outlines the payout structure, including details like the default payout amount, the currency, and payout scheduling” (https://help.impact.com/partner/what-would-you-like-to-learn-about/getting-started/sign-up-as-a-partner-on-impactcom ; https://help.impact.com/partner/what-would-you-like-to-learn-about/getting-started/getting-started-for-migrated-partners).
- **Product categories:** brands marketplace with “thousands of brands” (https://help.impact.com/partner/what-would-you-like-to-learn-about/platform-features/working-with-brands/applying-to-brands/join-the-brands-marketplace); documented asset types, e.g. “Image — This is a traditional banner-style asset” (https://help.impact.com/partner/what-would-you-like-to-learn-about/platform-features/marketing-content/brand-assets/asset-types-explained-for-partners); commissions can be varied by SKU, promo code, cart size or customer status via payout groups (https://help.impact.com/brand/what-would-you-like-to-learn-about/performance-program/introduction-welcome-to-impact.com/step-2-contracting-and-commissions).
- **Commission range:** brand-defined, not published by impact.com. Partners see “Commission amount ($) or commission rate (%) for an action / for the product”, plus AOV, average commission rate and EPC (https://help.impact.com/partner/what-would-you-like-to-learn-about/getting-started/getting-started-for-migrated-partners).
- **Cookie window / attribution:** the “Referral (or cookie) window” is a **contract-level setting — each contract between you and a partner defines its own referral window**; the window live on the action date governs; official examples include 15-day and 1-day windows (https://help.impact.com/brand/what-would-you-like-to-learn-about/platform-features/contracts-and-template-terms/referral-window-explained). Partners can read the Cookie Window per program in the Contract History Detail Report (https://help.impact.com/partner/what-would-you-like-to-learn-about/platform-features/reporting-for-partners/contract-history-reports/contract-history-detail-report). No single network-wide cookie duration (**UNVERIFIED** if you need one fixed number).
- **Payout methods (India):** (a) EFT Domestic = ACH, free, 3–5 business days, only where impact.com holds a bank account in your currency/region; (b) **EFT International = Wire**, 3–5 business days, “Currency-specific” fees; (c) **PayPal**, immediate, **2% processing fee (up to $20 or currency equivalent)** (https://help.impact.com/partner/what-would-you-like-to-learn-about/platform-features/finance/payments-withdrawals-and-balance/withdraw-funds-to-your-bank-account ; https://help.impact.com/partner/what-would-you-like-to-learn-about/platform-features/finance/payments-withdrawals-and-balance/withdraw-funds-to-your-paypal-account). “All payments require your SWIFT code” for non-US accounts (bank-details URL above). **Payoneer is not offered** on any impact.com partner payout page reviewed.
- **INR support:** “Indian Rupee | INR | **India, Bhutan, Nepal**” is listed as a supported currency for those bank-account locations, and **INR appears in the “Wire supported currencies” list** for partner withdrawals — an India-based partner can be paid by international wire in INR (https://help.impact.com/other/reference-documentation/supported-currencies-and-timezones). INR is not in the ACH list and not in the partner PayPal currency list (PayPal: USD, GBP, EUR, AUD, CAD, RUB, NZD, PHP, HKD, SGD, SEK, DKK, NOK, ILS, MXN, JPY) (https://help.impact.com/partner/what-would-you-like-to-learn-about/platform-features/finance/payments-withdrawals-and-balance/withdraw-funds-to-your-paypal-account).
- **Minimum payout threshold:** “Once you meet the prerequisites to be paid and your impact.com balance reaches **$10 USD** (or your currency equivalent), your funds become eligible for withdrawal”; autopay can be balance-threshold based (“as low as USD $10 or as high as USD $2.5m”, paid the following Tuesday/Thursday) or fixed-day (1st or 15th) — “even with this option, a minimum threshold of USD $10 applies before funds are withdrawn” (https://help.impact.com/partner/what-would-you-like-to-learn-about/platform-features/finance/payments-withdrawals-and-balance/how-do-partners-get-paid ; https://help.impact.com/partner/what-would-you-like-to-learn-about/platform-features/finance/payments-withdrawals-and-balance/partner-payments-explained-from-action-to-payout). Updating bank details triggers verification (up to 2 working days) and a 48-hour withdrawal pause (https://help.impact.com/partner/what-would-you-like-to-learn-about/platform-features/finance/payments-withdrawals-and-balance/how-do-partners-get-paid).
- **Signup requirements:** sign up with Apple/Google/email → verify mobile number → accept the Partner Program Agreement → categorise business (“Operating as — single individual or a company”) → connect channels, where “We recommend adding at least one verified property to increase your chances of getting approved to the impact.com marketplace” → submit the marketplace application for review; “Once you're approved, you'll see the partner dashboard and any pre-approved brands” (https://help.impact.com/partner/what-would-you-like-to-learn-about/getting-started/sign-up-as-a-partner-on-impactcom). Website verification is by email-domain match, impact.com tracking code, or impact.com's manual verification (generally 3–5 business days) (https://help.impact.com/partner/what-would-you-like-to-learn-about/account-management/account-settings/connect-media-properties/connect-and-verify-a-website). **No minimum traffic/audience figure is published (UNVERIFIED that a traffic floor exists).**
- **Pinterest relevance (officially documented):** “impact.com allows you to add a promotional channel automatically with your Instagram Creator (Professional) account, Tiktok, YouTube, Twitter/X, Facebook page, **Pinterest**, and Twitch accounts” (https://help.impact.com/partner/what-would-you-like-to-learn-about/account-management/account-settings/connect-media-properties/connect-a-social-media-profile). Pinterest OAuth permissions are documented: “See all your boards (including secret boards and board groups)” is used “to allow you to select deliverables from your boards for Pinterest campaigns”, and “Create new Pins and boards for you” is used “to create, and submit deliverables for Pinterest campaigns” (https://help.impact.com/partner/what-would-you-like-to-learn-about/account-management/account-settings/connect-media-properties/why-does-impactcom-require-social-media-permissions).
- **Creative/feed use:** “You can place a tracking link on your promotional sites or social media posts, or attach it to an image or text you've created”; brand assets (including Image/banner assets) are served from the Assets screen, and product catalog feeds exist to alert partners to newly updated brand content (https://help.impact.com/partner/what-would-you-like-to-learn-about/platform-features/tracking/tracking-links/create-and-manage-links/create-tracking-links ; https://help.impact.com/partner/what-would-you-like-to-learn-about/platform-features/marketing-content/brand-assets/manage-assets-as-a-partner ; https://help.impact.com/partner/what-would-you-like-to-learn-about/getting-started/getting-started-for-migrated-partners). **No official impact.com statement specifically about pinning brand or feed images to Pinterest — UNVERIFIED.**
- **India eligibility & tax:** impact.com publishes no country-restriction list; INR wires to India are supported (currency URL above), the strongest official signal that India-based partners are payable. Tax: “If you are US-based (**or not US-based but work with US-based brands**), an electronic tax document is required once your yearly earnings reach the IRS threshold of **$2,000 USD** in a U.S. tax year… impact.com cannot release payouts to you until your electronic tax document… has been submitted and approved”; “If you're a non-US partner working exclusively with non-US brands, you're not required to submit an electronic tax document”, but tax details must be correct, including **Indirect tax selection and Indirect tax number** (https://help.impact.com/partner/what-would-you-like-to-learn-about/platform-features/finance/payments-withdrawals-and-balance/how-do-partners-get-paid). **Indian PAN / GST / TDS are not named (UNVERIFIED); the indirect tax number field is the closest official analogue to a GST-type registration.**
- **Penalty risk to watch:** “If impact.com is unable to pay you for 6 months, a monthly account maintenance fee will apply starting in month 7… £25 GBP per month… or $10 USD per month… until you either 1) provide valid, updated tax or payment information, or 2) your account balance reaches 0” (https://help.impact.com/partner/what-would-you-like-to-learn-about/platform-features/finance/payment-requirements-explained-for-partners).

## 3. CJ Affiliate (cj.com)
- **Legal/network owner:** the Publisher Service Agreement is made with **Commission Junction LLC**, 530 East Montecito Street, Santa Barbara, CA 93103; “The applicable contracting party if you are located in the European Union or the United Kingdom is Epsilon International UK Ltd” — so a Kolkata-based publisher contracts with Commission Junction LLC (PSA page dated “Posted 21 August 2026”) (https://www.cj.com/legal/psa-us). CJ is part of Publicis Groupe — CJ states it contacts clients only from CJ.com, publicisresources.com or PublicisGroupe.net addresses (https://www.cj.com/support).
- **Product categories:** “By creating a free publisher account, you'll be able to review all available advertisers on our network. You're also able to see what each advertiser is offering for compensation as well as other program requirements.” (https://www.cj.com/support). The publisher page markets creator/content monetisation, social-reach syncing and sponsored opportunities (https://www.cj.com/publisher).
- **Commission range:** not published by CJ; “Transactions qualifying for a Payout are defined by the Advertiser. Advertisers may change Program Terms, including Payout rates, at any time upon written notice through the Network Service” (https://www.cj.com/legal/psa-us).
- **Cookie window / attribution:** CJ's developer documentation: “The action referral period is the amount of time you allow your cookies to remain active on your publishers' sites… **The standard referral period is 45 days; however, the cookie can be set from between 1 and 120 days.**” It is advertiser-configured in Program Terms, along with action-referral occurrences (number of commissions per cookie) (https://lab-developers.d.cjpowered.com/docs/data-imports/action-data). CJ also tracks without cookies via its click ID (cjevent) (https://lab-developers.d.cjpowered.com/docs/cj's-click-event-id-(cjevent)/http-cookie-method). Per-advertiser variation is the norm (**UNVERIFIED** that any single figure applies network-wide).
- **Payout methods (India):** direct deposit, check, or **Payoneer** — “Our partnership with Payoneer allows you to get paid in your local currency via your local bank account—without having to open an international bank account” and “Get paid in over 150 currencies via direct deposit or our global payments partner, Payoneer” (https://www.cj.com/publisher). The CJ–Payoneer programme covers “over 200 countries in 150 currencies”, justified specifically as expanding in “Asia, Latin America and Eastern Europe” (https://junction.cj.com/article/global-innovation-payoneer). **PayPal is not listed as a CJ payout option (UNVERIFIED as unavailable — it simply does not appear on CJ's publisher page or payment-cycle article).**
- **Minimum payout threshold (USD):** CJ's official publisher article: “change your Payment Method to direct deposit so you can change your Minimum Payment Amount to **$50** (vs the **$100 minimum for check**) and get paid more often” (https://junction.cj.com/article/konmari-your-cj-account-publisher-edition). The minimum payment amount is publisher-configurable under Account > Administrative Settings (https://junction.cj.com/article/cookie-dough-understanding-publisher-payment-cycle). **INR threshold equivalent is not published by CJ (UNVERIFIED).**
- **Payment timing:** locking cycles — standard locks on the 10th of the month after the Event Date, custom 7–60 days, or open-ended; closing occurs “around the 13th and 22nd of the month”; “Payments are made around the 20th (for all currencies) and around the 28th (for currencies USD, GBP, or EUR)”; outside USD/GBP/EUR there is no second-round payout, and CJ “cannot expedite or process any payments earlier than the two payment dates” (https://junction.cj.com/article/cookie-dough-understanding-publisher-payment-cycle).
- **Signup requirements:** free publisher account (https://www.cj.com/support). **A website is mandatory:** “Can I sign up without an active website? You will need an active website to submit your application to become a publisher. You will need to provide a valid URL, meaning the website must contain http://www. OR https://www., a valid domain, and a top-level domain” (https://www.cj.com/support). CJ also advises basic HTML/JavaScript ability for tracking implementation, and states “We do not sell or build websites. In order to be successful in our network, you'll need the resources to build and maintain your own website or blog.” (https://www.cj.com/support). Signup requires email verification and a Country field (https://signup.cj.com/member/signup/publisher/); individuals must be 18+ (“IF YOU ARE AN INDIVIDUAL, YOU REPRESENT AND WARRANT THAT YOU WERE AT LEAST 18 YEARS OF AGE”) (https://www.cj.com/legal/psa-us). Social profiles can be registered and carry their own tracking ID: “Add your social profiles to CJ… under Account > Websites > Promotional Property Manager… each social account will have a PID, or Promotional Property ID” (https://junction.cj.com/article/how-to-share-affiliate-links-on-social-media). **No minimum-traffic rule is published**, but each advertiser approves separately: “Upon approval by the Advertiser for acceptance into its Program, You may display… Links” (https://www.cj.com/legal/psa-us).
- **Pinterest use (officially documented, step-by-step):** “1. Choose the product you want to promote from one of your joined advertisers. 2. **Find and download the product image from the Links > Products page in your CJ publisher account.** 3. Save the image for your pin locally… 6. Add your affiliate link as the Destination Link… 9. Save!”, with the note “Due to Pinterest Affiliate Guidelines, the visible URL in the pin will be the first link redirect. In the case of affiliate links generated via CJ, this will appear as the CJ tracking domain.” (https://junction.cj.com/article/how-to-share-affiliate-links-on-social-media). This is the only one of the five networks whose official docs explicitly walk through downloading a product image and re-uploading it into a Pinterest pin.
- **Compliance guardrails:** FTC disclosure required; “Be careful posting anywhere you don't 'own'… Violating these policies may violate CJ's network quality standards and lead to account deactivation”; the PSA also prohibits placing links in third-party newsgroups, message boards, blogs, unsolicited email/spam, link farms, counters, chatrooms or guestbooks (https://junction.cj.com/article/how-to-share-affiliate-links-on-social-media ; https://www.cj.com/legal/psa-us).
- **India eligibility:** CJ runs India publisher development — its own blog reports “Reporting from CJ Bangalore, India, the Publisher Development team…” and lists a “Growth Manager, Publisher Development for India, Australia, and Southeast Asia” (https://junction.cj.com/article/q4-in-india-making-the-most-of-festivals-and-holiday-shopping-events ; https://junction.cj.com/article/your-new-best-friend-reaching-gen-z-in-australia-india-southeast-asia). CJ does **not** publish a country-eligibility list; the sign-up form carries a required Country field (**UNVERIFIED** — CJ's eligible/blocked country list could not be confirmed from official sources) (https://signup.cj.com/member/signup/publisher/).
- **Tax forms:** CJ maintains an official tax information page at https://taxinfo.cj.com/ (the page exists, content loads dynamically and could not be read). **The specific form for India (e.g. W-8BEN) is UNVERIFIED from official CJ sources.** PAN / GST / Indian TDS: not mentioned by CJ (**UNVERIFIED**).

## 4. Rakuten Advertising
- **Legal/network owner:** Rakuten Advertising (publisher Help Centre at pubhelp.rakutenadvertising.com; publisher contract = Publisher Membership Agreement and Network Policies; the official global office list shows Toronto, Shanghai, Sydney, Tokyo etc. and **no India office**) (https://pubhelp.rakutenadvertising.com/hc/en-us/articles/360060314292-Requirements-for-New-Publishers ; https://rakutenadvertising.com/en-uk/locations/).
- **Product categories / commission structure:** “Our advertisers set their offers, which vary from one advertiser to the next. Offers can include commissions based on a set percentage of sales, the number of impressions or click-throughs, or a combination of these. Offers can also be based on a flat fee for performing a certain action or generating a lead.” Three offer types: public, private, special (https://pubhelp.rakutenadvertising.com/hc/en-us/articles/360060325392-Offers-Overview). Performance is reported by advertiser vertical (https://pubhelp.rakutenadvertising.com/hc/en-us/articles/19649465948301-IAP-Trends-Top-Verticals-Tab).
- **Commission range:** not published; advertiser/offer-defined with CPS, CPC, CPM and CPA/flat-fee models (https://pubhelp.rakutenadvertising.com/hc/en-us/articles/360060325392-Offers-Overview).
- **Cookie window / attribution:** “Return days and cookie window have the same meaning… **Advertisers can set their own return days as a default for their program or at the offer level.** This information is available for review within the offers tab of each advertiser's profile page. The commission you earn is based on the terms of the offer in place at the time of purchase.”; the offer detail lists “Cookie Window — The number of days that can elapse between a referred customer's initial visit and a return visit…”; the **transaction update window defaults to 90 days** (TrueLock can shorten it) (https://pubhelp.rakutenadvertising.com/hc/en-us/articles/360059976411-Return-Days-or-Cookie-Window ; https://pubhelp.rakutenadvertising.com/hc/en-us/articles/360060325392-Offers-Overview).
- **Minimum payout threshold:** “you must earn more than the minimum commission for each network. The minimum needed per network is: Australia 50 AUD / Canada 50 CAD / France and Germany 50 EUR / UK 50 GBP / **US 50 USD**”; Brazil is exempt; this applies to every marketing channel and to every payment format (check, direct deposit, PayPal); if you do not reach 50 currency units within a calendar year, you are paid the outstanding balance at the end of Q1 of the next year; advertisers in different networks pay separately so the 50 must be reached per network (https://pubhelp.rakutenadvertising.com/hc/en-us/articles/4412254867981-Minimum-Payment-Threshold). Payments are issued weekly once funds arrive from the advertiser (https://pubhelp.rakutenadvertising.com/hc/en-us/articles/12477775647245-Reasons-You-Did-Not-Receive-a-Commission-Payment).
- **Payout methods (India) — the most explicit India support of the five:** per the Payment Options tables, **India (IN) sits in Group 1**, whose options are Check (US/CA/AU networks only, paid in network currency), **Direct Deposit — “paid in local currency” — available for all networks**, and **PayPal — “paid in network currency” — available for all networks except Brazil** (https://pubhelp.rakutenadvertising.com/hc/en-us/articles/360059980311-Payment-Options). Direct deposit to an Indian bank requires **IFSC code (11 characters) + account number (9–18 digits)** and “Your bank must be in the same country as your account address” (https://pubhelp.rakutenadvertising.com/hc/en-us/articles/4403950418445-Direct-Deposit-Required-Information). Rakuten staff confirm for India: “For an Indian bank account you do not need to enter the SWIFT code, only the IFSC code and the Account Number” (official help-centre staff reply, 10 Nov 2025) (https://pubhelp.rakutenadvertising.com/hc/en-us/articles/360049181191-Set-or-Update-Your-Payment-Method).
- **Payoneer / PayPal specifics:** “**Payoneer is not an available payment option.**” PayPal is available to publishers “in more than 200 markets” for the US/Canada/UK/Australia/France/Germany networks, must be PayPal-verified, pays in network currency (USD/EUR/CAD/GBP/AUD), needs the 50-unit minimum, and has per-transaction caps of 10,000 USD / 8,000 EUR / 12,500 CAD / 5,500 GBP / 12,500 AUD (https://pubhelp.rakutenadvertising.com/hc/en-us/articles/360059980311-Payment-Options ; https://pubhelp.rakutenadvertising.com/hc/en-us/articles/360059980411-PayPal-Payment-Option).
- **Cross-border constraint:** “Due to governmental restraints and taxation laws, we cannot send payments to any country other than the one declared in your account.” (https://pubhelp.rakutenadvertising.com/hc/en-us/articles/4402721021197-International-Banking-Information).

Inputs required: “a live website, blog, or customized social media page URL. This site should be live, not under construction”, plus “Details about your online presence” and “Tax information and a valid mailing address”; signup asks for URL, **number of monthly visitors and page views** and a business description — but “**Rakuten Advertising is an open network and does not screen publishers when they join**” (advertisers then apply their own criteria, e.g. minimum monthly visitors for a specific brand) (https://pubhelp.rakutenadvertising.com/hc/en-us/articles/360060314292-Requirements-for-New-Publishers ; https://pubhelp.rakutenadvertising.com/hc/en-us/articles/13214492487309-Requirements-for-Becoming-a-Publisher).
- **Pinterest relevance:** Rakuten Advertising is named as one of Pinterest's **official affiliate partners** (https://create.pinterest.com/blog/affiliate-marketing-link-basics/), and Rakuten's help centre gives Pinterest-specific steps: “Add your website URL to your Pinterest profile.
