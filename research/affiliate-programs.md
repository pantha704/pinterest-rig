## 0. Pinterest's 2026 affiliate rules that constrain which programs you can use

Verified against Pinterest's own policy page and creator docs (both live, re-checked 2026-09-22):

**0.1 Pinterest names four official affiliate partners.** "Pinterest isn't part of the affiliate agreements, so you'll need to choose an affiliate network. (**Our official partners are: Rakuten, LTK, Shop Style and Amazon Associates US.**)" — <https://create.pinterest.com/blog/affiliate-marketing-link-basics/>. This does **not** mean other networks are banned: Pinterest's *policy* page (below) contains no network allow-list, so Awin / Impact / CJ / Admitad / vCommission / EarnKaro links are permitted provided they comply with the Affiliate Guidelines. It *does* mean those four have documented, sanctioned Pinterest workflows — a real practical advantage when a link gets blocked.

**0.2 The link must go to a product detail page.** "The link should go directly to a shop's product detail page." — same source. **This kills home-page, category-page and search-page links**, and it is why "pin → product page" is the only compliant pattern for 2026.

**0.3 No link shorteners / no cloaking.** "Some shortener services are not currently supported on Pinterest. If the link on your Pin is blocked, remember that you can always edit it." — same source; repeated in the Affiliate Guidelines ("Be transparent about the commercial nature of your content and about your links and their behavior"). **Practical rule: use the network's raw tracking URL, or a network-generated deep link (e.g. Awin's MyAwin link builder). Do not wrap anything in bit.ly or a custom shortener.** Note that a network's own redirect domain (e.g. `...cj.dotomi.com` or an `awin1.com` link) is fine — CJ's official Pinterest guide even states "the visible URL in the pin will be the first link redirect. In the case of affiliate links generated via CJ, this will appear as the CJ tracking domain" (<https://junction.cj.com/article/how-to-share-affiliate-links-on-social-media>).

**0.4 Disclosure is mandatory.** You must follow the **Paid Partnership Guidelines** and "Include a clear disclosure to indicate the commercial nature of your content (such as the use of #ad …)" plus "Be transparent about the commercial nature of your content" — <https://policy.pinterest.com/en/commercial-and-branded-content-guidelines>. **Put `#affiliate` (and ideally `#ad`) in the pin description and/or on the pin graphic itself**, and check your affiliate program's own disclosure rule too (Amazon requires its exact sentence; Awin/CJ require FTC-style disclosure).

**0.5 One account, original content, moderation.** From the Affiliate Guidelines: "In general, you should operate only one Pinterest account. That account should be your authentic presence on Pinterest."; "Affiliate content should be original and add unique value for Pinners."; "Creating affiliate Pins repetitively or in large volumes — marketers should follow our spam policy and use affiliate links in moderation." — <https://policy.pinterest.com/en/commercial-and-branded-content-guidelines>. **Do not mass-publish 50 affiliate pins a day**; mix affiliate pins into a majority of genuinely useful non-affiliate content.

**0.6 Banned manipulation tactics** (same page): no fake accounts to create/save your affiliate links; no quid-pro-quo save exchanges; **no asking other users to create pins containing your affiliate identifier**; no paying people per save. Violations can get content *and accounts* removed or de-indexed.

**0.7 Product tagging is NOT available to you.** "Product tagging is available in US, CA, UK, DE, FR, IT, ES, AT, CH, NL, AU, BR, MX, JP." — <https://create.pinterest.com/blog/affiliate-marketing-link-basics/>. **India is not on the list**, so the India-based creator's pins must use **destination links** (which is allowed) and cannot use the native product-tagging/merchant flow. Do not plan a strategy that assumes product tags.

**0.8 Content-category bans that matter for a shopping account** (Commercial Content Guidelines): cannot promote/sell tobacco & vaping, marijuana, firearms/weapons, prescription-only medications or medical treatments; no excessive-alcohol depictions. **Fashion, home, beauty (non-Rx), food, stationery, tech accessories and decor are all fine.**

**0.9 Where the money is easiest to get out (India-specific ranking of payout rails).** This is the single most under-rated selection criterion, and it is why the ranking at the end of this document does *not* simply follow commission rates:

| Rail | Who pays that way | India reality |
|---|---|---|
| **NEFT/RTGS straight to an Indian bank account in INR** | Amazon Associates India (₹1,000), vCommission (₹1,000), EarnKaro (₹10), Admitad wallet (₹1,000) | Simplest; no FX conversion, no third-party wallet |
| **Direct deposit in local currency (INR) via IFSC** | **Rakuten Advertising** — India is in "Group 1", direct deposit "paid in local currency" across all networks | Excellent: an official Pinterest partner *and* INR-native |
| **Payoneer local-currency payout** | Awin (all cross-border payments go through Payoneer), CJ Affiliate | Works, but you must open a Payoneer account and Awin requires it to be created *from inside* your Awin account ("It is not possible to link an already existing Payoneer account to Awin") |
| **International wire in INR** | impact.com (INR listed as a wire-supported currency) | Works; currency-specific wire fees apply |
| **PayPal in network currency (USD)** | Rakuten (except Brazil), impact.com (2% fee, up to $20), Admitad | USD only → FX conversion on arrival |

## 1. Amazon Associates India (Amazon.in / in.amazon)

- **Network / owner:** Amazon Seller Services Private Limited (the Operating Agreement defines "We/us/our" as Amazon Seller Services Private Ltd). Program home: <https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/> — "Earn up to 10% in affiliate fees". Agreement: <https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/operating/agreement>
- **Product categories:** every Amazon.in category under the published fee schedule, minus exclusions. Fee schedule table (headed **Aug 2026**): Apparel/Shoes/Luggage/Watches/Beauty **10%**; Books, Toys, Personal Care Appliances, Baby, Automotive, Sports **5.9%**; Kitchen, Furniture, Home **5%**; Echo/Alexa, Fire TV devices **5%**; Grocery/Amazon Fresh, Health & Personal Care **4.7%**; Pet Products **4.7%**; Mobile Accessories **4%**; Large Appliances, Televisions, PCs/Smart Watches/Electronics **3.5%**; BISS/Lawn & Garden/Video Games **3.5%**; Bicycles & Heavy Gym Equipment **2.5%**; Data Storage **2%**; Mobile Phones **1%** (many flagship/Apple models 0%); Bill Payment & Recharges 30% capped ₹3; **All Other Categories 5%**. Bounties (flat ₹): Prime membership ₹100, Audible trial ₹150, Amazon Business ₹200, Sell-on-Amazon GST-validated ₹400, Prime Music first stream ₹100. **Excluded (no fee):** flight bookings, gift cards, video-game consoles/hardware, Kindle eBooks, gold & silver coins. → <https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/operating/advertisingfees/>
- **Cookie window:** **Session = 24 hours** from click-through; session ends on the *first* of (a) 24 h elapsing, (b) the customer placing an order, (c) the customer arriving via another associate's Special Link. Item must be **added to cart inside that 24 h session**, and the order placed no later than **89 days** after the initial click (cart lifetime ~90 days) for the sale to qualify. → <https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/operating/agreement> ; <https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/node/topic/G9SMD8TQHFJ7728F>
- **Payout method + threshold (India account):** **NEFT direct deposit to an Indian bank account (INR)** or cheque. Paid monthly, ~60 days after month end. Minimum: **₹1,000 for NEFT**, **₹2,500 for cheque** (you may raise your own cheque minimum). No invoice is expected by Amazon, but see TDS/GST caveat below. → <https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/node/topic/GKDG94FQSRXSJCGK> ; <https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/node/topic/GP8Z3AZ27ZFHTEUL>
- **Signup requirements:** free; you must declare at least one **Site** (website, mobile app or social page) and it is reviewed. Websites need robust original content (rule of thumb ≥10 posts), owned by you, recent (within ~60 days). Social pages need a **substantive organic following — in most cases ≥500 followers/likes** and must be public. **Accepted social networks are a closed list: Facebook (open group/fan page), Instagram, Twitter, YouTube, TikTok, Twitch.tv. Pinterest is NOT on the list.** You must also generate **3 qualifying sales within the first 180 days** or the application is not approved (personal orders don't count). **Rejected applications cannot be reassessed** — you must re-apply with a corrected site list. → <https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/node/topic/G8TW5AE9XL2VX9VM/>
- **Approval difficulty: HIGH for a Pinterest-only, no-site applicant.** With no accepted social network to declare, there is no compliant traffic source on the published rules. **Practical workaround:** declare an owned website/blog you actually control (≥10 original posts) as your Site, and use Pinterest as the *promoter* of that blog, with the affiliate link either on the blog post or deep-linked. Also create one accepted social profile (Instagram/YouTube) with ≥500 followers if you want a second declared site.
- **Official assets / product images in pins: NO — restricted.** The licence to copy/display Amazon Content (images, text, prices, widgets) is granted **"solely on your site"**, and Amazon Marks may be used only as part of Content and per the Trademark Guidelines. You may not add to, delete from or alter Content (resizing is allowed only with original proportions kept); you may not redistribute/sublicense Content, and you may not use Content on **any application, platform, site or service (including social networking sites) that requires you to sublicense rights in the Content**. → <https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/operating/agreement> ; <https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/operating/participation/>
  - **What this means for Pinterest in practice:** do not pin Amazon product photos from the Associates product-linking tool, do not pin Amazon price badges, and do not use Amazon's logo/Associates logo in pin graphics. Your pin should be **your own photograph/creative or a licensed stock image**, with your link going out. This is the single biggest compliance trap for a Pinterest-first Amazon strategy.
- **Disclosure required:** you must clearly and prominently state **"As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases."** → <https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/operating/agreement>
- **India caveats:** fee amounts are inclusive of all taxes; you must raise valid **GST-compliant invoices** and report them so Amazon can take input tax credit; Indian residents' fees are subject to income-tax **TDS** at the statutory rate; **PAN and tax information are mandatory** or fees are held in abeyance; non-residents need extra documentation; governing law is India, exclusive jurisdiction of courts at Bangalore. → <https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/operating/agreement>
- **Vintage note:** the Operating Agreement page still shows "Updated: November, 2021" but is the live official text; the fee schedule is headed Aug 2026 and help pages carry 2025–2026 footers.

**11.5 Can you pin Amazon product images?**
- The IP licence is: "we hereby grant you a limited, revocable, non-transferable, non-sublicensable, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to: (a) copy and display Program Content **solely on your Site**". **And "Site" is defined as** "your website, **social media user-generated content**, online software application, or Alexa skill". → <https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/agreement>
- Product Advertising Content must come from **Amazon's own tools** (Creators API/PA API or SiteStripe); **no alteration** ("you may resize Program Content consisting of a graphic image in a manner that maintains the original proportions"); **no caching of images** ("you may store a link to Product Advertising Content consisting of an image for up to 24 hours"); **every image must link to the relevant Amazon page**; and there is a hard transfer bar: "you will not… use, or enable, or facilitate the use of Program Content… on or within any application, platform, site, or service (**including social networking sites**) that requires you to sublicense or otherwise give any rights in or to any Program Content to any other person or entity." → <https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies>
- **Bottom line: a dedicated permission to place Amazon product images in PINS is UNVERIFIED.** No Amazon page names Pinterest either way. Because the licence is Site-scoped and tool-scoped, pinning Amazon product images is only defensible if the Pinterest profile is a Site Amazon accepted — and **Pinterest is not on the accepted-social-network list**. **Safest compliant pattern: create your own imagery for pins, and treat Amazon images as website/blog-only.** Amazon's own Pinterest integration is a *different* route requiring the **Amazon Influencer Program** + an **Amazon Storefront** + a Pinterest business account (<https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/connect-amazon-and-pinterest>); AIP is "a country specific program that is available in select countries" and **whether India is an AIP country is UNVERIFIED**.
- **Disclosure on Amazon pins is mandatory on the account itself:** "For social media user-generated content, this statement must be associated with your account", and link-level disclosure must be Clear ("(paid link)", "#ad", "#CommissionsEarned") and Conspicuous ("placed near any affiliate link or product review"). → <https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GPXFHVYZMTGPUMPE>

**11.6 The 180-day rules — get this exactly right, most guides get it wrong**
- **"3 sales in 180 days" is a PRE-APPROVAL review gate, not an ongoing requirement:** "Once you've applied, you have 180 days to refer a sale through one of your Associates links." / "Once you have referred three qualifying sales, we'll evaluate your application within a day or two and reply to you with our decision." / "Your application can be withdrawn if there were not enough qualifying sales for 180 days after your application was submitted. **We're unable to reinstate your account or Associates ID after it's been rejected.**" → <https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/G7MJTPEP9NC3YKMG>
- **After acceptance the published inactivity rule is dormancy, not a rolling sales quota:** "If you have not earned any commission income in the 3 years prior to any given calendar month… we may charge you an account maintenance fee… the lesser of $10 or the amount of unpaid accrued commission income. … Your account will be closed for dormancy when we take this action." → same URL
- **Any claim that the account closes for failing to keep making 3 sales per 180 days post-acceptance is UNVERIFIED** — that language does not appear in the October 2025 Operating Agreement or the April 2026 Program Policies. **Note the practical trap either way:** because of the *pre-approval* gate you must produce 3 qualifying sales **while still unapproved**, so you need real traffic on day one and mis-clicks won't do.

## 15. Signup-day runbook (what to have ready, in order)

**Have these open in tabs before you start:** PAN card image, a bank account statement or passbook page showing **account number + IFSC**, the mobile number you'll use for OTPs, a dedicated email address for affiliate accounts, and your Pinterest profile URL (plus an Instagram/YouTube profile URL if you have one).

1. **EarnKaro** — 10 minutes, no documents, ₹10 threshold. Do it first so you have *something* live and earning while everything else is pending. (<https://earnkaro.com/>)
2. **Rakuten Advertising** — "open network and does not screen publishers when they join", accepts a "customized social media page URL", and pays India by **INR direct deposit via IFSC**. Fastest *global* network to get into, and a named Pinterest partner. (<https://pubhelp.rakutenadvertising.com/hc/en-us/articles/13214492487309-Requirements-for-Becoming-a-Publisher>)
3. **vCommission** — self-serve, 2–4 business day screening, NEFT in INR, ₹1,000 floor. Apply with your Pinterest profile URL as the promotional space. (<https://www.vcommission.com/affiliate/signup>)
4. **Admitad** — register on the .in domain, then submit your Pinterest profile as an **ad space** and get it moderated *before* you apply to individual programs. (<https://www.admitad.com/in/>)
5. **impact.com** — create the account, verify phone, connect and verify the Pinterest channel, then apply to the marketplace. Watch the $10/month dormancy fee. (<https://help.impact.com/partner/what-would-you-like-to-learn-about/getting-started/sign-up-as-a-partner-on-impactcom>)
6. **Awin** — attempt registration; if the Tax Residency dropdown rejects India you'll know within a day. Make the promotional-space description specific ("Pinterest home-decor board with X saves"), never vague — vague descriptions are an explicit rejection trigger. (<https://success.awin.com/s/article/Why-was-my-network-application-rejected>) *(Note: Awin also requires Payoneer, and Payoneer must be created from inside Awin — budget an extra step.)*
7. **Amazon Associates India** — apply once you have a **declared site that is not Pinterest**: either a ≥10-post original blog you own (content within the last 60 days) or an accepted social profile (Instagram/YouTube etc.) with ≥500 organic followers. Immediately start working toward **3 qualifying sales in 180 days**, and keep pinning to <https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/node/topic/G8TW5AE9XL2VX9VM/> open to follow the rules exactly.
8. **Amazon Associates US** — only worth adding if you genuinely shop on amazon.com (gift-card payout, ≥$10) or can absorb USD cheque collection at ≥$100. Complete the **W-8BEN Tax Interview** in Account Settings and enter your **PAN as the foreign TIN** to try to cut the 30% withholding. (<https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GYJB2LE2AB473W2L>)
9. **CJ Affiliate** — gate it behind step 7's blog; CJ requires an active website. Its official Pinterest guide is the best asset-workflow documentation of any network here, so do the work when you're ready. (<https://www.cj.com/support>)
10. **LTK** — a Q1–Q2 2027 target, not a signup-day task: 5,000+ engaged followers on a public profile, content at least twice a week, 4+ months of history, and an Instagram account authenticated through a Facebook Business Page. (<https://company.shopltk.com/creatorapplication_expectations>)
11. **Skip entirely:** ShareASale (closed Oct 2025 → Awin) and ShopStyle Collective (closed Mar 2026).
12. **Tax housekeeping that applies across all of them:** keep your PAN current with every network (it is the withholding/TDS key almost everywhere — Amazon India and Admitad both state fees are held without it), take a GSTIN when your turnover requires it (Amazon India, Admitad and vCommission all require GST-compliant invoices in some form), and expect **TDS** — stated rates: Amazon India (statutory rate), vCommission (**1% individual / 2% company**), Flipkart (5% under S.194H), Admitad (prevailing Indian income-tax law). Keep a monthly commission statement per network for your ITR.
